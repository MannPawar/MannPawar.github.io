How people decide

What makes a player decide to quit, and do different minds choose to play in different ways?

The Silent Killers of Player Retention

Churn study of Steam’s top 50 games · briefed and reviewed by Kent Rollins (General Intuition, formerly Riot and Epic)

Reviews pulled through the Steam Web API and weighted by playtime to find where players quit. A local DeepSeek-R1 extracted 141 root causes across 47 games. Churn comes in walls: optimization debt before 15 hours, a veteran wall past 400 hours, and genre friction where working mechanics break the rules players expect.

Neurodiversity and Play

Survey reanalysis · 1,603 board gamers

A reanalysis of a 1,603-respondent board-gamer survey using PCA, latent profiles and equivalence tests, asking how neurodivergent and neurotypical players differ in what and where they play. The platform-preference model reaches a cross-validated AUC of 0.86.

How beliefs change

Can simple rules of belief and social influence explain how a thousand minds pick a side?

NPC Opinion Dynamics

Agent-based model of belief change in a game world

An opinion model of 1,010 Skyrim NPCs: each character's stance on the civil war moves with the people around them, their home hold and the battles, and the model was run 101,000 times and validated against an energy-matched null with Kolmogorov–Smirnov tests.

Try Grant Central Station

The prototype from my Pre-ICIS SIGDSA 2026 paper, running live. Give it a funding goal and it searches broadly, then ranks what it finds against the organization’s mission and programs.

  1. Connect. Add your own API key for a language model in the app’s sidebar. Searching needs one.
  2. Discover. Describe the funding goal, pick the countries, and add any funder sites of your own to the default sources.
  3. Re-rank. Grants found by earlier runs are re-ranked against the new goal and merged in, so each search builds on the last.
  4. Propose. Select a grant and generate a writeup in the proposal workspace.
Open the app

You can look around without a key, but searches run on a language model, so they need your own API key. The app runs on Streamlit Community Cloud. If nobody has used it for a while, it sleeps; wake it from the frame above and give it a minute.

About

I came to analytics from business management, and I still judge a model by whether someone can act on it on Monday morning. At William & Mary I finished the MSBA first in a class of 60, then spent the spring as an AI Fellow, building live demos and teaching faculty what these tools can and cannot do.

My research asks how people make up their minds, and how machine learning can model it. I study it through thousands of players’ own words, how neurodivergent and neurotypical players choose, and a thousand villagers in Skyrim, because games are the best laboratory I know for cognition.
